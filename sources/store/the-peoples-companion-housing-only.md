## Description (verbatim)

IMPORTANT NOTE: THIS IS THE 3D PRINTED HOUSING ONLY. electronics, battery, antenna, and radio module are NOT included.

The People’s Companion is a low-cost, solar-powered LoRa mesh companion radio for Meshtastic or Meshcore. THIS 3D printed housing is required to build a People's Companion.
The People's Companion is designed around affordable, reliable mesh components such as the Seeed Studio Xaio nRF LoRa module. The electronics stay inside the 3D printed housing, and on the exterior there is an SMA connector for you antenna, and an on/off rocker switch so you can disable your mesh device and conserve battery power for later.
Printed in PETG for heat resistance.

In addition to this housing, you must purchase the following parts to assemble a People's Companion:
Seeed Studio Xaio nRF LoRa Module
BFC 915 mHZ LoRa Antenna & SMA cable
18650 Battery Holder
18650 battery
On/Off Rocker Switch
