## Description (verbatim)

IMPORTANT NOTE: This BUILDER KIT includes all the components you need to assemble a People's Companion Mesh Radio, EXCEPT FOR THE 18650 BATTERY. Tools are also NOT included.

What's Including in the Kit?
1 People's Companion Housing (PETG material)
1 Seeed Studio Xaio nRF LoRa Module
1 BFC 915 mHZ SMA Antenna
1 10cm SMA to IPEX Cable
1 18650 Battery Holder
1 Mini On/Off Rocker Switch
The People’s Companion is a low-cost, solar-powered LoRa mesh companion radio for Meshtastic or Meshcore. THIS BUILDER KIT includes all the parts you need to assemble The People's Companion. The only part NOT included is the 18650 battery.
The People's Companion is designed around affordable, reliable mesh components such as the Seeed Studio Xaio nRF LoRa module. The electronics stay inside the 3D printed housing, and on the exterior there is an SMA connector for you antenna, and an on/off rocker switch so you can disable your mesh device and conserve battery power for later.
A small blue LED, is visible through the translucent housing. This is a visual indicator that the radio is currently powered-on and functioning.
Housing printed in Translucent PETG for heat resistance.

In addition to this Builder Kit, you must purchase an 18650 Battery to power your device. 18650 Battery Store is a good source for affordable batteries.

Required Tools:
Soldering Iron
Small crescent wrench
(helpful but optional) Tweezers
