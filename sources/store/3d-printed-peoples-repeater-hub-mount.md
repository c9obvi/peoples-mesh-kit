## Description (verbatim)

The People’s Repeater is a low-cost, solar-powered LoRa mesh repeater for Meshtastic or Meshcore. THIS 3D printed hub and mount system is required to build a People's Repeater.
The People's Repeater is designed around a $10 solar garden light from Lowes. The electronics stay inside the light’s original solar panel housing, while the printed hub gives you a solid place to mount the external antenna and turn the whole thing into a compact, weather-resistant mesh repeater.
Printed in ASA for weatherproofness.

IMPORTANT NOTE: THIS IS THE 3D PRINTED HUB AND MOUNT ONLY. Hardware, electronics, solar panel, battery, antenna, and radio module are NOT included.

Parts List:
Harbor Breeze Solar Garden Light
Seeed Studio Xaio nRf LoRa Module
Solar Charge Controller
18650 battery (comes with Solar Garden Light)
LoRa antenna & SMA bulkhead/pigtail
