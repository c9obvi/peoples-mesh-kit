## Description (verbatim, converted to Markdown)

The People’s Companion: A low-cost, LoRa mesh companion radio for Meshtastic or Meshcore.
The People's Companion is an affordable LoRa mesh companion radio build using reliable, low-cost parts like the Seeed Studio Xaio Development Kit and the Black Flag Civilian generic 915 mHz Whip Antenna. This is not a repeater, this is the type of mesh radio that you actually carry with you day to day. It's a simple design. You can assemble it at home in less than an hour using a just a few parts and common tools. Build list below.
The device runs for close to two weeks on a single 18650 battery, and thanks to the toolless entry, you can easily hot swap 18650's anytime. It has a mini on/off rocker switch, and an SMA connector so you can link it up with whatever antenna you prefer.

At the time of release, these companion builds cost approx. $25 plus shipping for parts.

Printer settings
I print most of my People's Companions in PETG. It's a good balance of affordability, heat resistance, and ease of printing. You may have success with other high temp filaments such as ASA or ABS, but I recommend you do NOT use PLA or PLA+. (PLA filaments will warp if they get even moderately hot.)

Material: PETG
Layer height: 0.2 mm
Layer thickness: 0.4 mm
Walls: 3-4
Infill: 30-40%
Supports: None
