# Your People's Companion: quick card

This little radio sends **encrypted text messages without cell service or internet**, using neighborhood mesh repeaters. Your phone is the screen and keyboard. The radio does the talking.

**Turn it on:** flip the rocker switch to **ON** (the side toward the outer edge). A blue light blinks inside.

**Connect your phone (one time):**

1. Install the **MeshCore** app (App Store / Google Play, or links at meshcore.io).
2. Open it → **Connect** → pick **MeshCore-…** (or the name your builder gave it).
3. PIN: **123456** unless your builder changed it. Change it in Settings.
4. Set your **name** and check the **radio preset** matches the group's: ______________________

**Use it:** post in **Public**, join the group channel ______________________, or send a direct message to a contact. Send important messages 2–3 times. Hold the radio up high or set it by a window for better range.

**Battery:** about **2 weeks** per charge. To swap, press the cap near the USB-C port and it pops off. Put the new 18650 in with **+ toward the + mark**. USB-C charging works with the switch ON but is slow. A spare cell is easier.

**Care:** **not waterproof.** Keep it dry and out of a hot car. Only use the antenna it came with, screwed on finger-tight.
