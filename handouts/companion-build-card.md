# Companion build card (bag "C")

Full steps: GUIDE.md Part C · Video: youtu.be/RTBM0CscnLI from 14:00

**In the bag:** enclosure + cap · XIAO kit (pre-flashed **Companion BT**) · antenna + 10 cm cable · battery holder · rocker switch · 18650 (stays in its case until step 7)

- [ ] **1** Holder wires out the end slots → through the cable hole → into the center clip. Press the holder in.
- [ ] **2** SMA through its hole from inside. Washer + nut outside. Snug with the wrench.
- [ ] **3** Boards apart. IPEX onto the **Wio** board: flat, press, click. Give the coax one 360° twist. Wio into its pocket.
- [ ] **4** Switch in, **OFF side toward the inside**. Cut the red wire just past the switch posts. **Keep the piece.**
- [ ] **5** Strip all 4 red ends a few mm and twist.
- [ ] **6** Tin the BAT pads under the **XIAO** (not touching!). Black → **BAT−**, red jumper → **BAT+**. XIAO back on, USB-C down. Reds → the two switch posts. Heat shrink.
- [ ] **🔍 Helper check:** pads not bridged, red goes to +.
- [ ] **7** Cell in (+ end right!). Cap on. Antenna on. **Then** switch ON. Blue blink = good.
- [ ] **8** Flash station (if not pre-flashed) → **9** pair in the app (PIN 123456) → **10** say hi in the group channel.
